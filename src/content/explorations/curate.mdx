###### Summary

###### Opening up the market for investing and buying art to younger investors.
The prompt read: create an app that helps art buyers. It was general and vague. Amid the depressing COVID headlines, one piece of news caught my eye— A 22% decrease in global art sales, but online sales reached $12.4 billion. COVID saw the rise of NFTs and online art sales. 

This project is my version of making art investing accessible to younger generations.
