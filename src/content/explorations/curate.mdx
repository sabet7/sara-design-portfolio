###### Summary

## Opening up the market for investing and buying art to younger investors.

The prompt read: create an app that helps art buyers. It was general and vague. Trying to find some inspiration, I read the newspaper, and amid headlines about COVID I found an interesting headline— A 22% decrease in global art sales, but online sales reached $12.4 billion. COVID saw the rise of NFTs and online art sales. This provided an opportunity to make art buying more accessible.
