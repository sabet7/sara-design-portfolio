###### Summary
As part of a hackathon hosted by Liftyz and Design Buddies, I worked with a team of designers across the U.S. to update IKEA's current buyback/resell program. The initial site page presented problems for the program. Working as a researcher and designer in the group, I turned to Reddit for a user sentiment analysis. From the insights from the sentiment analysis, I learned that users were frustrated by the slow pace and multiple redundant steps within the program (verifying and vetting the item and meeting IKEA staff just to have their item rejected at the last minute). Working within IKEA's current design system, I implemented AI into some of IKEA's features, hoping to make the resale program easier.   

#### Pain points

![Current user flow of resell program](/media/explorations/ikea/ikea-user-flow.jpg) 

- Program had too much user friction
- The start page of the buyback program was located in an unexpected place. 

The program seemed disjointed from its purpose— to reduce furniture waste by 2030.
