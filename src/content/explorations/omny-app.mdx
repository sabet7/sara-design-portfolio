###### Summary
In 2023, the MTA announced that it would phase out the iconic MetroCard by 2026. When the OMNY card was introduced, it brought a lot of concerns regarding privacy and data. Various consumers reported that their personal bank cards had been overcharged or that double fares had been taken from their OMNY cards.

![User sentiment analysis of OMNY cards](/media/explorations/omny/user-analysis.png "New Yorkers took to Reddit to express their frustrations with the new fare card. Many mentioned difficulties reloading cards, double charges, and accussed the card of stealing money from bank accounts.") 

While customers have gotten used to the change, there are still issues and concerns. I ideated a mobile app that replaces the OMNY.info website and make managing paymets, trips, and balances a bit easier. 

###### Project Details
I was inspired by Japan's SUICA digital card for this project. While physical smartcards have become common, there is always a risk of running out of chips to make the cards. Providing a digital option can be a great low cost way to keep the card in circulation and also make it accessible to anyone with a smart phone.
