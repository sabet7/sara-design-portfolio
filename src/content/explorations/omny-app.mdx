###### Summary

In 2023, the MTA announced that it would phase out the iconic MetroCard by 2026. When the OMNY card was introduced, it brought a lot of concerns regaridng financial data privacy. Many consumer reported that they were overcharged or that the fare did not grant them transfers. Over the years the service has improved, but customer satisfaction and brand reputation have taken a big hit.

As a result, I ideated what the OMNY card should look like, an hommage to the original MetroCard which has become an iconic symbol of New York.
