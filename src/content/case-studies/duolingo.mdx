Few students enjoy science classes. This is something I’ve witnessed firsthand while tutoring biology to high school students. Rote memorization and abstract concepts that don't feel connected to everyday life can make learning any science subject uninteresting. Sadly, low engagement and interest have caused low science scores on the Regents exam, with only 44% of students achieving proficiency.
